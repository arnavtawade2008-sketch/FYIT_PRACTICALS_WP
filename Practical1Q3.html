Technology Definitions

Artificial Intelligence(AI)

Technology that enables machines to simulate human intelligence and perform task such as learning and decision-making.

Cloud Computing

Delivery of computing services such as storage, servers, and applicatons over the Internet.

Cyber Security

Protection of computer systems, networks, and data from cyber threats and unauthorised access.

Internet of Things(IoT)

A network of interconnected devices that collect and exchange data through the internet.

Blockchain

A distributed digital ledger use to securely record transactions across multiple systems.
