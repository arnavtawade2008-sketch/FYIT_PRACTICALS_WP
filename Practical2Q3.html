Introduction

Emerging Technologies are transforming industries and creating new opportunities. Technologies such as Artificial Intelligence,Cloud Computing,Cyber Security and Internet of Things(Iot) are driving innovation worldwide.

Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) enables computers to perform tasks that normally require human Intelligence such as learning, reasoning and decision-making.

Cloud Computing

Cloud Computing provides on-demand access to computing resourses such as servers, storage and applications over the internet.

Cyber Security

Cyber Security protects computer systems, networks and data form cyber threats and unauthorized access.

Internet of Things

Iot connects physical devices to the internet, enabling them to collect anmd exchange data.
